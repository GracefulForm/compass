Orienteering Course (Beginner)

An orienteering course requires you to download and print a map and use a baseplate or other map compass. See our Getting Started page for help.

Orienteering Map

This is a standard orienteering map that includes the north lines, control points, and bridges. The bright pink north line is provided in case you are attempting to do this with a phone.

The maps on this page do not display well on phones. They are meant to be printed.

Scoring

There are several styles of orienteering courses. Some of them track the time it takes to speed to each control point. Others my use scoring points for correctly reaching each flag. In the answer boxes on the maps, you can choose how you want to do the course.

Safety Reminders

  • Be aware of your surroundings.
  • Be courteous with the disc golfers.
  • Watch your head and step while walking with a map and compass.
  • Drinking fountains and restrooms are located near the starting sign.

This course will give you approximately 1.5 miles of walking exercise.
