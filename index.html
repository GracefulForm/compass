Introduction

What are compass and orienteering courses?

A compass course is an activity where you are given a compass direction and distance to travel to reach a goal. This is similar to a pirate treasure map.

An orienteering course is an activity where you use a compass and a map to decide how to get from one location to another.

There are 3 courses at J.M. Caldwell Sr. Community Park:

Beginning Compass Course

This course will help you to learn how to work with a compass and count your paces to reach different stopping points. It is great for beginners, young adventurers, and families.

Intermediate Compass Course 

This course starts out on the Beginning Compass Course, to warm up, and then adds difficulties, such as destinations that are not visible or on the other side of barriers and need to be reached in different ways.

This can be thought of as a cross between a compass course and an orienteering course.

Beginning Orienteering Course

This course will help you to get started using a map and compass to find your "bearings" and reach a distant destination.

You will need to print a map to properly follow an orienteering course.
