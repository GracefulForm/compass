How To Measure Your Pace

Watch ashort training video by Dr. Eric LaMalfa.

From the compass course sign to the back left corner of the restroom is 130ft.

Example:

  • I counted 26 paces as I walked from the compass sign to the back left corner of the restroom building.
  • I calculated 130 ft. ÷ 26 and my answer was 5 ft.
  • Now, as I walk my compass course, I know that each pace (every other step) is about 5 ft. If I want to walk 200 ft., I will count 40 paces to get there since 40 paces x 5 ft. = 200 ft. 

How To Find Your Compass Direction

Watch a short training video by the MRCA Authority.

Watch a longer training video by Texas Parks & Wildlife.

If you are using a phone, hold your phone straight in front of you and turn yourself until your phone is showing the correct direction that you want to go.

If you are using a compass:

  • Turn your dial until the direction that you want to go is at the top or lines up on your direction arrow. 
  • Turn your body until your red compass needle (Fred) is inside the red triangle (Shed.) 
  • Keep Fred in the Shed as you walk the direction that you want to go.

How To Find A Location Across A Barrier

  1. Go as far as you can go until you reach your barrier, such as a creek or deep crevice.
  2. Find a landform or landmark on the other side of the barrier that lines up with your direction. Study it closely so that when you go around the barrier, you can go to it and continue in the direction that you want to go.
  3. Go around the barrier, find your landmark, and then continue following your compass direction.
